This project analyzes customer behavior to identify factors affecting retention and churn using statistical and mathematical tools in Python.

***Key Insights***

-PurchaseAmount shows slight skewness but sample means follow normality due to CLT.

-No statistically significant difference in spending between genders or campaign groups.

-Certain product categories show higher churn tendencies.

-Regional variation in spending patterns observed through ANOVA.

-The 95% confidence interval provides a reliable estimate of the true mean purchase value.
